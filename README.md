### Mikhail Serikov

Quantitative modeller: optimization, forecasting and decision-making under uncertainty for energy markets, power systems and climate, infrastructure and financial risk.
M.Sc. Ocean and Climate Physics, Universität Hamburg; thesis on the value of learning about ocean heat uptake in a climate-economy model (GAMS, Python).

My self-directed research studies on public data are grouped by topic:

- **[Power markets](https://github.com/serikov-power-markets)**: price forecasting, PPA valuation, battery and gas storage, supplier hedging and portfolio risk
- **[Energy systems](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems)**: adequacy across weather years, PyPSA and TIMES models, grids, bidding zones and district heating
- **[Climate risk](https://github.com/serikov-climate-risk)**: windstorms on the transmission grid, floods and heat on transit networks, loss models and damage functions

#### Credit and insurance
- **[credit-pd-report](https://github.com/michaelserikov/credit-pd-report)**: PD scorecard against gradient boosting, rating grades and validation, SQL, Excel/VBA and Power BI reporting, mortgage PD and LGD through the 2007-2010 crisis, IFRS 9 expected credit loss
- [motor-pricing-glm](https://github.com/michaelserikov/motor-pricing-glm): motor liability pricing, GLM against gradient boosting, large claims and premium risk under reinsurance

**Tools:** Python (pandas, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch), GAMS, Pyomo, PyPSA, HiGHS, SQL (DuckDB, PostgreSQL), Excel/VBA, Power BI, Git and GitHub Actions
